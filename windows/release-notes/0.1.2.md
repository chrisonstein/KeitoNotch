- The notch no longer vanishes when you pick a project, task or client from a list, and stays on top of maximised windows.
- Dragging works: the tab or its peek card follows the pointer and snaps into place when you let go.
- The switches in Settings react to a click again.
- Ticket first: a new entry starts in the Jira ticket field (your open tickets and the ones you logged recently). Picking or pasting a ticket fills in the project and task. Optional ticket rules in Settings → Jira. Ctrl+V with a Jira link in the day view starts an entry for it.
